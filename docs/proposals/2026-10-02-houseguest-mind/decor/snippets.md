## Spec consts (`room.rs`, after `CAT_BED`)

```rust
const PLANT: Spec = Spec {
    name: "potted plant",
    pitch: "So green and leafy!",
    footprint: (3, 3),
    ascii: &["\\|/", "~Y~", "\\_/"],
    ink: (Color::Rgb(122, 166, 106), Color::Green),
    uses: &[],
    offers: &[],
    sit: None,
    comfort: 1.0,
};
const POSTER: Spec = Spec {
    name: "poster",
    pitch: "It'd look nice up!",
    footprint: (4, 2),
    ascii: &[".--.", "|~~|"],
    ink: (Color::Rgb(247, 184, 154), Color::LightRed),
    uses: &[],
    offers: &[],
    sit: None,
    comfort: 1.0,
};
```

ASCII as rendered:
- Plant: `\|/` / `~Y~` / `\_/`, a leaf fan over a pot.
- Poster: `.--.` / `|~~|`, a sheet with a picture.

Both are mirror-safe: `room::mirror` turns each row back into itself, and neither uses `< > { }`.

Inks:
- Plant: the front-leaf green #7aa66a, which is `Rgb(122,166,106)`; 16-colour `Green`.
- Poster: the peach sky #f7b89a, which is `Rgb(247,184,154)`; 16-colour `LightRed`. The sky is the largest flat area of colour in the piece.

## Critique and what changed

A separate art-director agent reviewed the work. It saw only the PNGs: renders at 1× over #1e2127, plus the 1× pixels scaled up nearest-neighbour. It reviewed twice, and there were three revisions: v1 was a self-check, v2 went to critique 1, v3 went to critique 2, and v4 is final.

**Round 1 (v2)**

Plant problems: it read as a snake plant or aloe, the drooping leaf made the pot look lopsided, the pot had no taper at 1×, and the green was too saturated. Changes:
- Broader, blunter leaves, about 1:2.5.
- The drooping leaf was replaced by two low broad leaves.
- The rim was centred and the pot tapered more.
- The greens were muted to #7aa66a (front) and #608a58 (back).

Poster problems: it read as a Polaroid, the 3° tilt smeared every edge into gradients, the sun had no contrast against its band, and the whale tail was a fleck that merged with the sun. Changes:
- Axis-aligned, with even borders and a soft-red title band where the photo margin was.
- Paper warmed to #f3ead8.
- An orange half-disc sun on the right.
- A bigger, notched whale tail on the left, with a pale splash line.

**Round 2 (v3)**

Both pieces read at 1×. One real issue remained: the poster's image rectangle sat half a pixel off the grid, so every inner edge blended and the paper went grey-beige. Fix: every poster edge now sits on a whole pixel at a 9 × 19 cell.
- x moves in steps of 20/9 units.
- y moves in the same steps, starting at −0.44, where a plain 4 × 2 frame puts its first row. The rasterizer is width-limited at scale 0.45, with dy = 0.2 px.
- The exact 1× render is now crisp.

Polish:
- Poster: the sky moved toward peach (#f7b89a) to separate it from the red band, and the tapes were angled to ±30° on the corners.
- Plant: one side leaf moved to break the mirror symmetry, and the back green was lifted so the top tip doesn't melt into the background.

v4 was not sent for a third critique.

## Open notes

- **Poster pixel snap.** It assumes a 9 px cell and a frame exactly 2 rows tall. The props sheet's floor-standing render sits about 0.5 px lower, so faint blended rows show there. When the poster gets its real wall placement, render it the way the throwaway `decor_exact` test did and check that dy.
  The throwaway test, for `art.rs` tests (run with `HOUSEGUEST_DECOR=/dir cargo test -p dessplay --lib -- --ignored decor_exact`):

  ```rust
  #[test]
  #[ignore = "writes PNGs for review"]
  fn decor_exact() {
      let dir = std::env::var("HOUSEGUEST_DECOR").expect("HOUSEGUEST_DECOR");
      for (prop, name) in [(Furniture::Plant, "plant"), (Furniture::Poster, "poster")] {
          for s in [1u32, 2] {
              let (cols, rows) = prop.spec().footprint;
              let (w, h) = (u32::from(cols) * 9 * s, u32::from(rows) * 19 * s);
              let pad = 9 * s;
              let mut sheet = image::RgbaImage::from_pixel(
                  w + 2 * pad,
                  h + 2 * pad,
                  image::Rgba([30, 33, 39, 255]),
              );
              let image =
                  render_prop_layer(prop, Layer::Whole, Facing::Right, LINE, w, h).unwrap();
              image::imageops::overlay(&mut sheet, &image, i64::from(pad), i64::from(pad));
              sheet.save(format!("{dir}/{name}-exact-{s}x.png")).unwrap();
          }
      }
  }
  ```

  The snap also holds at HiDPI 18 × 38 cells, because 0.9 × (20/9)k = 2k.
- **Tape margin.** If the wall frame differs from 80 × 84, recheck the tapes' margin to the top of the frame. They currently clear it by about 2 px.
- **Outline (unfilled) mode.** It shows the leaves' hidden overlaps. The critic judged that no worse than the other furniture.
- **Parcel.** The critic suggested a cardboard tube for the poster instead of the generic box. This is optional.
